**Question:** What is our policy on weekend deployments?

**Answer:**

Officially forbidden, but it has been overridden at least once — the policy
and practice disagree.

The engineering handbook states that production deploys are not made between
Friday 16:00 and Monday 09:00. A mail thread from 14 March shows Dana
approving a Saturday deploy for the invoicing hotfix, calling it "an exception,
not a precedent", over Sam's objection that the rule should hold. No document
records this exception; it exists only in mail.
